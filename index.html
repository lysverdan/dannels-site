Concept notes

What this concept assumes, and what to swap or confirm before building it for real.

  • Mark Dannels is mentioned once, as founder, the same way the current site does. How he is credited or remembered is the owner's call.
  • All the text uses facts from the current site, except the items below. The gem drawings are illustrations; photos of real pieces replace them.
  • The nine pieces in the case and the "Season Opening Preview" invitation are samples.
  • The repair types on the envelope are typical jeweler services. Keep the ones Dannels actually offers.
  • "Private appointments, including weekends" is a suggestion. Reef Gallery is open seven days and Dannels is weekdays only, so weekend appointments would close that gap. Keep it only if she will offer it.
  • Jewelers of America lists the store as established 1985, with custom design, appraisals, engraving, and a bench jeweler. If those are current, add "Since 1985" to the home page, add appraisals and engraving to Services, and move custom design out of "Coming soon."
  • The email signup here is a demo. On the real site it needs a storage option connected in Squarespace.
  • "Prices are shared privately with Members" assumes prices stay off the site. Change it if she wants to show them.
  • The look can be rebuilt in Squarespace with its fonts, colors, and page settings. The animations are optional extras.
